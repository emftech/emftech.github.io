## Teaching

I have been a teaching assistant at PUCP for 10 consecutive semesters, across 9 courses, since March 2022. I lead lab and practice sessions, and design, grade and give written feedback on weekly labs and exams in MATLAB and Python, often for up to four courses in parallel.
