## Estefano Machado Ferrer
<p class="subtitle">Biophotonics and Biomedical Optics Research Group</p>
<p class="subtitle">Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP)</p>

Electronics Engineer (B.Sc., PUCP) specialized in digital signal and image processing. I am a researcher in the Biophotonics and Biomedical Optics Research Group at PUCP, where I work on **optical coherence elastography (OCE)**: using optical coherence tomography to image how mechanical waves travel through soft tissue, and turning those measurements into quantitative maps of tissue stiffness.

My interests sit where medical imaging meets signal processing: wave-based elastography, inverse problems, and image reconstruction and estimation methods for biomedical data. I also teach as a Teaching Assistant in the Faculty of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at PUCP.

In my undergraduate thesis, supervised by Prof. Fernando Zvietcovich, I proposed **Synchronized BM-mode (SBM)**, a new acquisition protocol for wave-based OCE that reconstructs 2D wavefields in equivalent time from ordinary raster scans. I validated it in simulation against multimeridian MB-mode scanning and implemented both protocols on swept-source and spectral-domain OCT systems.
