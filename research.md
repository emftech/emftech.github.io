### Thesis

<ul class="entries">
  <li>
    <span class="title"><a href="https://github.com/emftech/OCE_AcquisitionProtocols_Simulator/blob/main/docs/Machado_2026_Thesis_Acquisition_Protocol_NonCylindrical_WaveBased_OCE.pdf" target="_blank">Development of an Acquisition Protocol for Non-Cylindrical Wave Propagation in Wave-Based Optical Coherence Elastography</a></span>, 2026 <span class="cat">B.Sc. thesis</span><br />
    <span class="authors"><span class="me">E. Machado Ferrer</span>. Advisor: Prof. Fernando Zvietcovich. <span class="venue">Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú.</span></span>
    <details>
      <summary>Summary | <a href="https://github.com/emftech/OCE_AcquisitionProtocols_Simulator/blob/main/docs/Machado_2026_Thesis_Acquisition_Protocol_NonCylindrical_WaveBased_OCE.pdf" target="_blank">Thesis PDF</a> | <a href="https://github.com/emftech/OCE_AcquisitionProtocols_Simulator" target="_blank">Simulator</a> | <a href="https://github.com/emftech/OCE_Experimental_Protocols" target="_blank">Experimental code</a></summary>
      <p>Wave-based OCE estimates tissue stiffness from the speed of mechanical waves, but most scan protocols assume waves spread cylindrically from the source. The thesis proposes Synchronized BM-mode (SBM), a raster BM scan locked to a harmonic excitation so that stacked frames sample each pixel in equivalent time, with per-pixel phase compensation and 2D phase-gradient speed estimation. In k-Wave simulations with stiff inclusions and simulated eye motion, SBM kept a higher contrast-to-noise ratio than multimeridian MB mode and reached comparable spatial resolution with a much shorter acquisition. Both protocols were implemented on swept-source (200 kHz) and spectral-domain (50 kHz) OCT systems.</p>
    </details>
  </li>
</ul>
